// ONGOING PROJECTS //

404 ARCHIVE

404 ARCHIVE documents the personal homepages of mid-'90s and early-'00s transgender, transsexual, and other gender-diverse communities via a network of hyperlinks recovered from the Wayback Machine.

Camp Trans Oral History Project

The Camp Trans Oral History Project (CTOHP) preserves firsthand accounts of Camp Trans--the two-decade-long annual protest of the Michigan Womyn’s Music Festival’s trans-exclusionary “womyn-born-womyn” policy—-and its longstanding impact on trans, lesbian, and feminist communities from the 1990s to today.

Misfiled, Unnamed: Transgender Misrepresentation & Archival Violence at Columbia & Barnard's University Archives

Misfiled, Unnamed locates trans material in the midst of Columbia and Barnard’s archives in order to understand how and why trans material ends up in an institutional university archive—and what that can tell us about the relationship between the university and trans communities at large.

Bergen Street Community Garden Oral History Project

The Bergen Street Community Garden Oral History Project records the 50-year history of a community garden in Crown Heights, Brooklyn through oral history interviews and archival research.
